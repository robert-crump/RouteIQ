  Room's runtime schema validation requires an entity's declared columns to exactly match the live
  table, and the real export's exact column list isn't known here - so GraphAssetDao uses @RawQuery
  (schema-agnostic) instead, and GraphTable is a closed enum of the 8 documented table names to keep
  table-name interpolation safe.
  - GraphAssetRepository exposes getGraphStats() (row counts across all 8 tables) and getMetadataRows()
    (generic column-agnostic read of the metadata table). More targeted queries (edges by node, turns
    by junction, spatial lookups) were left for the modules that will actually need them
    (RouteGraphMatcher etc., per issue #1) rather than guessed at now.
  - GraphStatsScreen (Compose) wired into MainActivity shows the counts end-to-end.

  in @Before (matching the 8 documented table names) rather than shipping a binary fixture, since the
  real cycling_graph.db wasn't available on this machine to derive one from.
